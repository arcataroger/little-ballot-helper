How to read this guide
  1. Each race lists every option in ranked order. A filled oval marks the current pick.
  2. "Why" explains the pick. Every claim links to its source. A claim with only one source says unconfirmed.
  3. "Your call" races are ones where sources this guide trusts disagree. Pick an option and the card updates.
  4. "The other side's best case" gives the strongest argument against the pick, in one sentence.
